Our Story

Where the town comes
to feel its best.

Face2Face began with a simple belief — that everyone in Suratgarh deserves the kind of grooming experience usually reserved for the big cities. Today, our family salon brings together skilled hands, considered design and genuine warmth under one roof.

From a quick gentleman's cut to a full bridal transformation, every guest is met with the same quiet attention to detail.
