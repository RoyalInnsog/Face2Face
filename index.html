Our Story

Where women come
to feel their best.

Face2Face began with a simple belief — that every woman in Suratgarh deserves the kind of beauty experience usually reserved for the big cities. Today, our salon and academy bring together skilled artists, considered design and genuine warmth under one roof.

From an everyday blow-dry to a full bridal transformation — and the courses that teach the craft — every guest is met with the same quiet attention to detail.
